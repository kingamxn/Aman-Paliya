TRAFFIC CODE

// STATE OF SAN-ANDREAS · LSPD & SAHP ADMINISTERED · EDITION 04/30/2021

🔍
Article 1. General Provisions
T.C 1.1The Traffic Code is an official set of rules relating to road traffic safety, registration of road vehicles, licences and permits, under the administration of LSPD and SAHP of the State of San-Andreas.
T.C 1.2The Traffic Code is mandatory for all residents and visitors of the State of San-Andreas.
T.C 1.3Violations of the Traffic Code are subject to penalties such as: a fine, seizure of a vehicle, detention, imprisonment and revocation of the drivers license.
T.C 1.4All road offenses are determined by a special policy, where the accused is charged for every vehicle implied in every road incident with their participation.
T.C 1.5No one can be fined twice under the same article for a single traffic incident.
T.C 1.6The officer has the right to conduct a visual inspection of the vehicle and verify all documents, licenses and registrations during a pull-over. Inspection of closed compartments (trunk, glove compartment) is allowed if it takes place in front of the driver, by free consent of the driver, and if the driver opened the closed compartment.
T.C 1.7The officer has the right to check the documents of the vehicle driver only. Exception: active vehicle or passenger wanted listing.
T.C 1.8The driver is obliged to provide documents at the first request of a law enforcement officer.
T.C 1.9The driver is obliged to stop at the first request of a law enforcement officer.
T.C 1.10The driver and passengers are required to remain in the car stopped by a law enforcement officer. It is prohibited to leave the car without the officer's request. Failure to comply is regarded as an assault threat and allows the officer to open fire to neutralize the suspect.
Article 2. Registration Rules
T.C 2.1The State license plate with valid registration is mandatory for all vehicles. Absence of a valid licence plate is punishable by a fine of $5,000
T.C 2.2Every vehicle has an easily accessible Vehicle Identification Number (VIN) in the upper left part of the instrument panel visible through the windshield. Damaging the VIN number presupposes a search of the vehicle and evacuation to the Penalty Parking lot, with the possibility of buyout at the maximum rate.
T.C 2.3An insurance of a vehicle is optional, it is a private choice of one's owner. It cannot be imposed and cannot be revoked.
Article 3. Traffic Rules
T.C 3.1Speed limit on the whole territory of the State: 120 km/h — Highway: 180 km/h — Special Zones: 60 km/h. Exceeding the speed limit is punishable by a fine of $5,000. Exceeding in Special Zones: $10,000.
T.C 3.2Compliance with the established direction of traffic and road markings is mandatory for all vehicles.
T.C 3.2.1Driving the opposite lane of the road: $10,000
T.C 3.2.2Driving the opposite lane of the highway: $15,000 + imprisonment up to 30 months
T.C 3.3Stopping on the road for no reason and ignoring officer's demands to continue driving: $5,000. Exception: vehicle malfunction.
T.C 3.4Cars left in places not established by Article 7 shall be towed to LSPD impound lot. Buyout rate: $10,000
T.C 3.5Dangerous driving is a criminal offence where the driver is alleged to have driven in a manner dangerous to the public.
T.C 3.5.1Driving on deserted sidewalks / bike paths: $3,000
T.C 3.5.2Chaotic movement between lanes: $5,000
T.C 3.5.3Failure to keep the distance: $3,000
T.C 3.5.4Hard braking: $5,000 (Exception: accident avoidance)
T.C 3.5.5Creating an obstruction for overtaking: $5,000
Article 4. Emergency Vehicles
T.C 4.1Drivers of emergency vehicles may activate flashing lights or a siren in performance of their duties. When activated, they may benefit from exemptions from certain traffic rules as long as maneuvers can be done without danger.
T.C 4.2Emergency vehicles are: police vehicles, ambulances, and fire safety vehicles.
T.C 4.3An emergency vehicle driver with lights & siren activated is not required to follow: road signs / traffic lights, prescribed speed limits, road markings, indicators for turns/lane changes, the ban on zigzag passes, the ban on passing in opposite lane near intersections, the ban on overtaking on the right, nor the prohibition to immobilize on a sidewalk, limited access path, or prohibited area.
T.C 4.4When an emergency vehicle is approaching with lights/siren on, all road users must: give way, reduce speed, stay to the right of their lane, and stop when required to ensure safe passage.
Article 5. Special Zones
T.C 5.1A Special Zone is a geographic area defined by special conditions prevailing over the general Traffic Code laws.
T.C 5.2A Special Zone covers the named geographic location and its adjacent territory within a radius of 30 meters (100 ft).
T.C 5.3The speed limit in the Special Zone must be 60 km/h at all times.

Article 6. Parking Rules
T.C 6.1Parking along roadways, a driver must park a vehicle in the direction of traffic with wheels parallel and next to the curb.
T.C 6.2Parking of vehicles is prohibited: on curbs marked in red, when interfering with other vehicles, when blocking exit, at crosswalks, if more than 2/3 of sidewalk is blocked, on dividing strips, against direction of traffic, on bridges or tunnels, on highways or freeways, closer than 20m from a building on fire, at bus stops, on railway tracks, in yellow parking spaces, in violation of road markings, and on lawns. Exception: official state organization transport.
T.C 6.3Violation of parking rules: $5,000 and possible evacuation to Penalty Parking lot.
T.C 6.4Unauthorized presence of vehicles on Prison territory (exception: parking lot at entrance): $15,000 + evacuation to Penalty Parking lot.
